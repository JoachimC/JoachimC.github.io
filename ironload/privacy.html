IronLoad privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

The short version

IronLoad collects nothing about you. Your equipment and your training log stay on your phone.

What the app stores

The app keeps your equipment sets, the weight you last asked for, and your settings. All of it stays on the device. None of it leaves the device unless you share an equipment file yourself. Your phone's own backup can copy these, the same as for any other app.

The app also keeps your training log: your exercises, your sessions, and each set with its load, reps, reps in reserve, the time you logged it, your bodyweight if you give it, and your notes. This is personal data, so here is what happens to it.

  • It stays on the device. The app sends none of it anywhere.
  • It is not in your phone's backup. The app keeps it out on purpose, so no copy of it sits in a cloud backup.
  • If you lose the phone or remove the app, the training log is gone. Nobody can get it back, including us.

The app has no accounts, no sign in, and no analytics. It does not know who you are.

What leaves the device

Two things, and only when you start them.

A tip. If you tip, the payment goes through Google Play or the App Store. That store handles the payment and tells IronLoad only whether it succeeded. IronLoad never sees your card, your name, or your address.

An equipment file you share. If you share a set, the app writes a file and hands it to whichever app you pick. Where it goes from there is up to you and that app. The file holds your equipment and nothing else.

Nothing else. There is no server behind IronLoad.

The app has no permission to use the network at all. Two Google parts in the Android app, the QR code reader and the tip, normally send usage logs about themselves to Google. IronLoad turns that off, so they send nothing. The QR code reader works on the phone.

Children

IronLoad is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone, so it holds no children's data.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app. Everything it stored goes with it, the training log included. There is nothing on a server to delete.

Changes

Any change to this policy appears here, with the date above.

  • 18 November 2026: the app gained a training log. It stays on the device and is kept out of the phone's backup.
  • 18 November 2026: the Android app no longer lets Google's QR code reader and billing parts send usage logs to Google, and it has no network permission.

Getting in touch

Write to help@quack.zone.

We hold nothing about you, so there is no data to correct or to delete. If you want the equipment or the training log you saved removed, remove the app.
