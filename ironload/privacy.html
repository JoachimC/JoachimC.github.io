IronLoad privacy policy

Last updated 14 September 2026.

The short version

IronLoad collects nothing about you. Your equipment stays on your phone.

What the app stores

The app keeps your equipment sets, the weight you last asked for, and your settings. All of it stays on the device. None of it leaves the device unless you share an equipment file yourself.

The app has no accounts, no sign in, and no analytics. It does not know who you are.

What leaves the device

Two things, and only when you start them.

A tip. If you tip, the payment goes through Google Play or the App Store. That store handles the payment and tells IronLoad only whether it succeeded. IronLoad never sees your card, your name, or your address.

An equipment file you share. If you share a set, the app writes a file and hands it to whichever app you pick. Where it goes from there is up to you and that app. The file holds your equipment and nothing else.

Nothing else. There is no server behind IronLoad.

Children

IronLoad is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone, so it holds no children's data.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app. Everything it stored goes with it. There is nothing on a server to delete.

Changes

Any change to this policy appears here, with the date above.

Getting in touch

Write to help@quack.zone.

We hold nothing about you, so there is no data to correct or to delete. If you want the equipment you saved removed, remove the app.
