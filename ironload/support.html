IronLoad support

IronLoad works out how to load a barbell, a dumbbell or a cable machine to the weight you want. It runs on the phone alone. It needs no account and no network.

Get help

Write to help@quack.zone.

One person answers this address, so please allow a few days.

What to tell us

A short message helps more than a long one. These four lines usually cover it.

  • The phone and the version of iOS or Android.
  • The version of IronLoad. The settings page shows it at the foot.
  • The equipment set you used, and the weight you asked for.
  • What you expected, and what the app showed instead.

Common questions

The app shows a weight below my target. IronLoad picks the highest weight it can reach that is not above your target. The red figure under the total shows how far short it falls. Add a smaller plate to the set if you own one.

The weight on the plates does not match my target unit. A set can mix units. IronLoad shows the number printed on the plate first, then the same weight in the unit you read. Change the unit you read on the settings page.

My gym has different plates. Edit the equipment set, or make a new one. The presets are a starting point, not a limit.

Does the app track me? No. See the privacy policy.

Report a problem with your data

IronLoad holds your equipment on your phone and nowhere else. We hold nothing to correct or to delete. If you want your equipment removed, remove the app.
